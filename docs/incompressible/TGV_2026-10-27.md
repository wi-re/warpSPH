# Native TGV investigation, 27 October 2026

The overnight IISPH stop is reproducible, and the other native TGV runs are
not accurate enough to qualify merely because they complete. The shared
operator's conditioning, a permissive divergence criterion and the retained
near-lattice sampling all matter. No IISPH or DFSPH default is changed by this
investigation. The normal matrix remains the original measured result.

[Figures, videos, exact metrics and every attempt](../../.tmp/tgv-pressure-investigation-2026-10-27/index.html)
are retained locally, including failed and deliberately stopped controls.

## IISPH failure

The instrumented 256², GPU FP32 replay matches the matrix's final positions,
velocities, densities, pressures, masses and supports **bitwise**. It stops at
4.781 s with max speed 72.46 m/s. At that point mean sampled density is
1.35624ρ₀; 16.45% of particles have another particle within 0.25dx.

For relaxed Jacobi, an eigenmode of D⁻¹A is multiplied by 1−ωλ per iteration.
At ω=0.25, positive λ must stay below 8. Fixed-state 320-step power iterations
on the actual native operator give:

| Saved state | Largest eigenvalue | Approximate stable ω limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Initial fixture | 4.2724 | 0.4681 |
| Before t=4.778 s, 1 ms timestep | 7.9383 | 0.25194 |
| Before the failing t=4.781 s step | 8.10469 | 0.24677 |
| Failing half-timestep control | 8.45976 | 0.23641 |

The failing 1 ms state's eigenvector residual is about 1e-6. Its high mode
grows by 1.02617 per iteration, so continuing to the 100-iteration cap amplifies
the error. Pressure reaches approximately −4161 to +3160 in normalized units;
the returned residual is larger than the input-iterate residual reported by
the stopping statistic. This is a measured linear instability, not a guess
based on the movie.

Halving dt delays failure only to 4.8405 s. The timestep guard then rejects a
request below minDt after an enormous pressure acceleration. Relaxation 0.1
delays the velocity stop to 4.9893 s, with sampled mean density 1.46544ρ₀ and
no cap on its final step. Lower relaxation alone does not repair the spatial
state. The half-dt error path subsequently segfaulted during cleanup; its
recorded error, prefix, snapshots and encoded movie remain preserved.

The recent spray-neighbour cutoff is not the relevant fix: this failure is
in dense periodic bulk, and the signed solve intentionally bypasses the
free-surface 0.9ρ₀ gate. The published IISPH formulation clamps negative
pressure to limit exaggerated cohesion. Our signed, mean-source periodic
variant has different semantics; clustering is consistent with that risk,
but this study does not isolate tensile forces from quadrature/conditioning
effects. A safer linear solve and a physically justified periodic sampling
policy remain separate requirements.

## Initialization and divergence criterion

The current prepared fixture has fourfold order Q4=0.999740. It is a quiet
near-lattice, not a glass. The separately saved raw-rearranged/cooled fixture
has Q4=0.256752. Its duration-limited preparation flag is retained; it is not
relabeled fully relaxed. Both fixtures use identical nominal particle masses
filling the periodic domain and sample the velocity field afterward.

Independent analytic TGV advection of the near-lattice particles, with no
SPH dynamics, produces mean kernel density 1.14069ρ₀ by 5 s. The continuum
velocity remains divergence-free: this is sampling/quadrature bias, not proof
of physical compression or lost mass. Halving the RK4 advection step from
0.01 to 0.005 s changes the measured mean-density results by less than
1.3e-11. Forcing the kernel density toward a target on a deforming lattice
can therefore create substantial corrective dynamics.

The reference-style DF tolerance is a per-step density percentage divided by
dt. At dt=1 ms, 0.1% permits mean normalized rate error of 1 s⁻¹, comparable
to this flow's velocity gradients. The overnight classic DF stage consequently
uses one iteration at every step. The matched tolerance/IC controls keep the
density solve, viscosity and physical inputs fixed:

| IC | DF tolerance | Energy-curve error | Velocity L2 at 5 s | Mean ρ/ρ₀ | DF caps | Wall / engine ms per step |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Near-lattice | 0.1% | 6.669% | 21.28% | 1.10932 | 0 | 45.09 s / 6.50 |
| Near-lattice | 0.001% | 4.971% | 8.69% | 1.02864 | 35 | 56.98 s / 8.86 |
| Near-lattice | 0.0001% | 4.493% | 4.66% | 1.00598 | 716 | 199.36 s / 37.28 |

| Rearranged | 0.0001% | 1.481% | 2.67% | 1.00491 | 527 | 178.86 s / 33.20 |

Energy-curve error uses the matrix's existing relative L2 metric; analytic
final K/K₀ is 0.81873. The best control reaches 0.79159. Capped records are
not fully converged solutions. A final budget-only check on that candidate
still accumulated 118 DF cap hits with a 1000-iteration budget by 0.651 s;
it was deliberately stopped at 0.786 s, rather than presented as a completed
fix. Its explicit stop decision, video, scalar prefix and error-path cleanup
segfault are preserved. Simply spending more Jacobi iterations is not yet a
qualified remedy.

These are classic-order controls of the shared operator/criterion. The other
two orderings have not been requalified at these tighter tolerances. The
original VD+PS TGV already uses 0.001%, so its original weak result cannot be
attributed solely to the looser 0.1% setting.

## Work and viscosity

In kinetic-energy units per unit depth, the matched loose near-lattice run
accumulates DF work +6.709 and density-correction work −6.149; viscosity removes
3.781. With tighter DF at 0.0001%, those become −0.165, −0.012 and −2.203.
The rearranged/tight case gives −0.042, −0.008 and −2.007. Finite-kick quadratic
terms are included. The observational work replay is bitwise identical to
the matrix endpoint.

Fixed-geometry viscosity checks distinguish the coefficient from numerical
velocity noise. At the loose run's final positions, the recorded velocity
loses energy viscously at 1.63 times the analytic rate; the exact TGV field
evaluated at those same positions gives 0.92 times. For the tighter near-lattice
run these ratios are 1.26 and 0.98. At initialization both are about 1.055.
Reducing viscosity to compensate would hide flow/particle errors rather than
isolate their cause.

Next work is operator-aware linear-solver conditioning and a periodic IC/
sampling acceptance criterion, followed by all-ordering qualification at a
declared rate-error bound. The source-centered constraint remains an explicit
experimental evolution variant; it does not conserve absolute sampled density.

The reusable [prepared-state probe](../../scripts/probe_preparedProjection.py)
records explicit numerical overrides with the normal matrix's video and
watchdog machinery. Raw diagnostics and the work/spectrum drivers remain in
the local report directory with their source receipts.
