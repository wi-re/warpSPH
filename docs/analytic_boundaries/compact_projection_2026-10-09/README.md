## What it does for the schemes

* **Inviscid Taylor-Green vortex** (periodic, no walls; `scripts/probe_tgvCompactProjection.py`, nx 48, `divergenceFree`; the energy of a flow that should lose none), KE / KE0 at t = 2: jacobi 0.9914, **compact 0.9982** (4.8x less loss), compact without the density solve 0.9935 (the warpSPH VD+PS shift of `finalize` instead of DFSPH2D's `fixed` shift: A3). Frames `tgv_t3_jacobi_over_compact.png`: regular lattices, both stable.
* **Does it lift the relaxation limit? No, not where it matters** (`scripts/probe_omniOmegaLimit.py`, still analytic tank, 150 steps, `omega_limit.log`): at n_h 4, omega 0.5 diverges with either divergence stage; omega 0.4 blows up with the Jacobi divergence stage (9e5 m/s) and stays bounded (1.7 m/s) with the compact one. So part of the limit sits in the three divergence sweeps (the compact stage removes that part), the rest in the clamped density solve, which stays Jacobi: the p >= 0 clamp makes it nonlinear and a Krylov solver there failed already (DFSPH_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN Part 43). At n_h 2.57 all of omega 0.3 - 0.5 are stable with the Jacobi stage.
* **Free surfaces: not an improvement, as in the boundaries repo.** Still sloshing tank with the compact divergence stage: peak |v| 1.0 - 2.4 m/s (Jacobi 0.2 - 0.5), rising at n_h 4. The oracle's own free-surface column study behaves the same: `scripts/studies/dfsph_freesurface.py column --variant compact` (robin wall, free surface): near-wall pressure error 2.9, mean-height drift -22 dx, vmax 7.3; its `mirror` and `compact-diff` variants stay bounded in the short run but have a bulk pressure error of 0.58 (mirror) and the difference variant crashes (NaN) in the study. The oracle's own notes keep free surfaces on the omniSPH-style path ("the mirror projection has the right hydrostatic pressure but surface noise / splash clusters", item 6 open), so the `mirror` wall and the `difference` / `renormalised` gradients are **not ported** here: they are an unfinished research branch of the oracle. The compact projection is the closed / periodic-flow tool.
* **Cost** (eager torch CG, sloshing tank N ~ 4200): 65 CG iterations per step at tol 1e-8, 4.8 s for 100 steps against 5.2 s for the Jacobi stage, so the Warp / graph CG of the oracle is not needed at this size (revisit for larger N).
* Boundary-particle scenes refuse it (`NotImplementedError`); only analytic walls and periodic / closed domains (no walls) are supported.

## A2 / A3: closed preset, gauges, Fickian shift

* `modules/shifting/fickian.py`: `particleShift = 'fixed' | 'fickian'` (schemeConfig `shifting`, `shiftA`): `dx = -D grad C`, `grad C = sum V_j grad W + wall G`, `D = shiftA h_s^2` or `shiftA h_s |v| dt`, tangential part only in the free-surface layer (surface particles and neighbours), cap 0.25 spacing; equal to `DFSPH2D`'s shift to 3e-5 (`scripts/probe_omniFickianOracle.py`; with the layer; without it 2 % from the cap, which the oracle takes as 0.25 PACKING h and warpSPH as 0.25 dx). The Verlet list carries pairs beyond the support: the layer must count only pairs inside it (that was the whole difference between 20 % and 3e-5). It replaces the VD+PS shift of `IncompressibleSystem.finalize` (switched off when the Fickian shift is on) and is refused with boundary particles.
* `closedPreset` (`True` | `'auto'` | default off) fills `projection='compact'`, `densitySolve=False`, `particleShift='fixed'`, `shiftA 0.5`, `divergenceGauge='min'` where they are still at their default (`resolveClosedPreset`). **Off by default here** (DFSPH2D turns it on for fully periodic domains): the shipped TGV would change behaviour.
* **The preset does not carry over to warpSPH's TGV.** Inviscid TGV nx 48: compact + density solve + VD+PS 0.9982 at t = 2 (best), compact, no density solve, VD+PS 0.9935, the full preset (fixed shift, no VD+PS) 0.9543 and max |v| 1.6. Isolated at t = 1: compact without density solve and without any shift 0.9960 (VD+PS on), the same with the `fixed` or `fickian` shift (VD+PS off) 0.984. The Fickian shift is a weak regulariser at warpSPH's time step (dt ~ 1e-3, thousands of steps; DFSPH2D's `fixed` D is per step) and removing VD+PS costs energy. The preset stays opt-in until a closed walled case (the channel group) measures it; the TGV recipe is `projection='compact'` with the density solve and the shipped VD+PS shift.
* A4 (free-surface Dirichlet of the projection, `rho < surfaceRho`) came with A1; it is the oracle's rule, not the detector's.

## A5 / A6: moving walls, loads

* **Moving walls (A5).** Nothing new was needed in the solvers: `_solve` takes the body velocity through `wallDivergence(bodyVelocity=True)` in the density stage's source (and in the divergence stage with `analyticWallInDivergence`, and always in the compact projection), the wall pressure force already clamps the pressure it sees at the wall (`p >= 0`, the oracle's `wallDivergenceClamp`: fluid pairs keep the signed pressure), and the incompressible systems integrate the prescribed bodies. A tank translating at U = 0.5 m/s with the fluid at rest in the lab frame: after 150 steps (t = 0.28 s) the body is at U t, the fluid follows (mean vx / U 0.62 and 0.56 for omni with the wall out of / in the divergence solve, 0.62 and 0.54 for `divergenceFree`), max |v| 0.88 - 1.47 m/s, no suction. Test `test_a_translating_tank_carries_the_fluid_with_it`. Free (dynamic) bodies are refused by the incompressible loops (the load is booked, the body is not integrated from it).
* **Loads (A6).** `schemeConfig.analyticWallLoads = True` books `RigidBody.load` ([2, 3] = (pressure, friction) x (Fx, Fy, torque)) for `omniIncompressible` and `divergenceFree` from the wall accelerations the solves applied and the friction impulse (`_bookWallLoads`, `computeBoundaryFriction(perBody=True)`). On a still tank the booked vertical load is as noisy as the density-solve pressure (0.5 - 1.2 of the weight from step to step) and averages to the weight of the fluid within 8 % over 40 steps (measured 0.96); the no-penetration impulse and XSPH are not booked.

## B: the VD+PS shift of `IncompressibleSystem.finalize` on analytic walls

Before: wall-blind (it ran only without gravity, so no analytic case reached it). Now: with a boundary provider the constant-density iteration is the wall-aware `omniIncompressible._solve` (density, alpha, source, pressure force all carry the wall; same iteration as `solveIncompressible`, min 3 / max 256, tol 1e-3, `dvdt = 0`), and the velocity-gradient resampling gets the wall's free-slip term `(u_b - v)_n n (x) G` (mirror bodies: the full jump). Sign / index convention of `gradVel[n, i, j] = d_j v_i` checked on a stretch field (test). Boundary-particle scenes keep `solveIncompressible`.
Gate (`scripts/probe_vdpsClosedTank.py`, `divergenceFree`, no gravity, nx 100 tank 1000 particles, smooth swirl 0.15 m/s with zero normal velocity at the walls, 1 s): analytic walls KE/KE0 0.948 / 0.946 / 0.945 / 0.937 / 0.919 at t = 0.2 ... 1.0, density 0.99 - 1.010, no flier (`vdps_closed_tank_t0_0.5_1.0.png`: the rows shear along the streamlines, a free-surface top row at 0.76); the boundary-particle run of the same case **gains** energy (KE/KE0 1.27 at t = 1, rho max 1.015), so the analytic path is the better-behaved one here. Test `test_vdps_shift_runs_on_analytic_walls_without_gravity`.

## C: `iisph` and `dfsphReference` on analytic walls

Hooked into `schemes/dfsphReference.py` (both steps; `iisph` is `dfsphReference` with the divergence pass off): `_factor(wall=)` adds `2 S . G + |G|^2` to the DFSPH factor, `_drhodt(wall=, bodyVelocity=)` the wall flux `-rho0 sum_b (f_b - f) . G_b`, `_pressureAccel(wall=)` omniSPH's symmetric wall form (weight 1, the weight the scheme's `akinciBoundaryVolumeScale = 2` gives its wall particles), the divergence solve's particle-deficiency guard counts the wall as the neighbours it replaces (`lambda` x the bulk count); the XSPH / boundary-friction / loads options work as in the omni loop. Moving and free bodies are refused (the divergence solve runs on advected positions with the bodies at the old pose). `runner.ANALYTIC_WALL_SCHEMES` now has all five; ACSPH stays refused; both are flagged EXPERIMENTAL.
* **Operator check** (`test_the_operator_diagonal_is_the_dfsph_factor_with_the_wall`, no oracle exists for these schemes): the diagonal of `A p = dt^2 drhodt(a_p(p)) / rho0` equals `dt^2 x factor` to 0.943 on the first row (0.465 without the wall term in the factor), 0.999 and 1.000 on the second and the bulk.
* **Still tank, 300 steps, peak |v| [m/s]** (analytic / boundary particles of the same scheme): `iisph` 1.99 / 0.79 (n_h 2.57), 6.2 / 1.14 (n_h 4); `dfsphReference` 1.33 / 0.59 and NaN after ~250 steps / 0.86. Both are the scheme's own known weakness, not the wall: the full carry of `kappa` degrades the free surface (rho min 0.33, then the "uniform soup" of DFSPH_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN Parts 29-31; with the warm start halved `iisph` n_h 2.57 stays at 0.47 and rho min 0.67). Neither is a candidate for sloshing; `omniIncompressible` / `divergenceFree` are.
* **Dam break** (`scripts/probe_incompressibleAnalyticDambreak.py --scheme iisph`, front / mean height / max |v|, omniSPH in brackets): t = 0.2: 0.347 / 0.267 / 3.20 (0.303 / 0.263 / 2.82); 0.4: 0.968 / 0.091 / 4.25 (0.918 / 0.087 / 3.67); 0.6: 1.287 / 0.088 / 8.17 (1.297 / 0.079 / 6.97): a faster front than the omni loop (no divergence pass), no alarm to t = 1. `dfsphReference` blows up after the wall impact at t ~ 0.83 (|v| 1e8, rho min 0.33), the same late-time failure its docstring documents for boundary particles.
